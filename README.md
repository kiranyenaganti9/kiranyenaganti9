# Kiran Yenaganti

Founder of DataGovernAI LLC and doctoral student in Information Technology at the University of the Cumberlands.

I research AI runtime governance and multi-agent systems. My work examines how approval scope, current evidence and execution conditions can be checked together before an agent acts, and when a changed condition should require human review.

## Read the research

- [Evidence-bound Runtime Governance for Autonomous Multi-agent AI Systems: Policy Enforcement, Monitoring, and Risk-based Human Escalation](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7622081)
- [Agent-Gov-Bench: A Benchmark for Governance Continuity in Autonomous AI Agents under Runtime Failures](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7623298)
- [Manuscript versions and research status](https://www.kiranyenaganti.com/research/)

These manuscripts are preprints presenting proposed designs and evaluation protocols. Empirical evaluation remains part of the research work ahead.
